Renting under 25 — what you need to know

How old do you have to be to rent an exotic car with GKHT?

Most of our fleet rents to drivers 18 and over. 29 of our 46 vehicles are available at 18+, and another 7 at 21+. A small number of vehicles carry a 25+ minimum, and those are marked on the individual vehicle page.

Do you charge a young driver fee?

No. The daily rate you see on the vehicle page is the rate you pay, regardless of your age. There is no under-25 surcharge.

What do I need to rent at 18?

A valid driver's licence, full comprehensive and collision insurance in your name, and a refundable security deposit. Deposits are listed on each vehicle page and typically range from $500 to $1,500.

Which cars can I rent under 25?

36 of our 46 vehicles, including Lamborghini, Ferrari, McLaren, Rolls-Royce and Porsche. Every car listed on this page is available to drivers under 25 — the exact minimum for each one is shown in its spec table.

Do you deliver?

Yes. We bring the vehicle to your hotel, home, or venue across Phoenix and Scottsdale, Los Angeles, Miami, and San Francisco.

How do I book?

Call or text (213) 845-5211, or use the booking form on our contact page. We confirm availability, insurance and deposit before you collect the car.

Most exotic rental companies won't hand you the keys until you're 25.
