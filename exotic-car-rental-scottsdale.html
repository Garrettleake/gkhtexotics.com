What you need to know

How much is exotic car rental in Scottsdale?

Rates start at $250/day for our promo-rate BMW M4 and run to $1,850 for the McLaren 720S. Each vehicle page shows the exact daily rate and deposit.

Do you deliver in Scottsdale and Old Town?

Yes — we deliver across Scottsdale, Old Town, Phoenix, Paradise Valley, Tempe, Mesa and Chandler, and to Sky Harbor and Scottsdale Airport.

Can I rent an exotic in Scottsdale under 25?

Yes — many of our Scottsdale vehicles rent at 18+ or 21+ with the right insurance. See our under-25 page for the full list.

Do you rent for Barrett-Jackson week?

Yes, and it books out early. Reserve well ahead for auction week and other peak Scottsdale events to lock in the car you want.
