How Much Does It Cost to Rent an Exotic Car?

GKHT Exotics · Phoenix, Scottsdale & Los Angeles

Exotic car rental pricing looks confusing from the outside, but it comes down to a daily rate plus a refundable deposit, with a few possible extras. At GKHT Exotics, daily rates currently run from $250 to $2,300 depending on the car, the city and the season. Here is what actually drives the number.

What a day costs, by tier

  • Entry exotic and luxury — roughly $250 to $650/day. Our promo-rate BMW M4 in Scottsdale, the Mercedes S580, Cadillac Escalade, and BMW 3 and 5 Series in LA live here.
  • Mid supercars and SUVs — roughly $650 to $1,000/day. Corvette Z06, AMG G63, Aston Martin Vantage, Range Rover, Tesla Cybertruck.
  • Top exotics — roughly $1,000 to $2,300/day. Lamborghini Huracán and Urus, Ferrari 488 and 296 GTB, McLaren 720S, Rolls-Royce Ghost, Cullinan and Spectre.

Every car’s exact rate is on its vehicle page. If budget is the priority, our most affordable rentals are gathered on one page, sorted low to high.

The deposit is not a fee

Alongside the daily rate you pay a refundable security deposit — typically $800 to $2,000 — held against damage and returned after the car comes back clean and on time. It is not an added cost, but you do need the room on your card for it.

What else can affect the total

  • Mileage. Rentals include a daily mileage allowance; extra miles are billed per mile. If you plan a long drive, ask about the cap when you book.
  • Delivery. We deliver across our metro areas, usually at no charge. Delivery well outside the metro may carry a fee.
  • Taxes. Arizona rentals carry transaction privilege and county tax; these are shown at booking.
  • Season. High-demand weeks — Barrett-Jackson, big events — book out early and hold firm on price.

There is no young-driver surcharge at GKHT. An 18-year-old with the right insurance pays the same daily rate as anyone else.

How to keep it affordable

Pick from the lower tier, keep the rental to the days you actually need the car, stay inside the mileage allowance, and book mid-week or outside event windows. Our affordable exotic rentals page is the fastest way to see what fits a tighter budget. When you know what you want, send a request and we’ll confirm the all-in number within 24 hours.

Common questions

How much does it cost to rent an exotic car?

At GKHT Exotics, daily rates run from about $250 to $2,300 depending on the vehicle, plus a refundable security deposit of roughly $800 to $2,000. Each vehicle page lists its exact rate and deposit.

What is the cheapest exotic car you can rent?

Our lowest daily rate is the promo-rate BMW M4 in Scottsdale at $250/day. Several luxury cars start around $445 to $450. See our affordable rentals page for the full low-to-high list.

Is the security deposit refundable?

Yes. The deposit is held against damage and returned after the car is returned on time and undamaged. It is not an added fee.

Are there hidden fees?

No hidden fees. Beyond the daily rate and refundable deposit, the only possible extras are mileage over the included allowance, delivery far outside the metro, and applicable taxes — all disclosed before you book.
