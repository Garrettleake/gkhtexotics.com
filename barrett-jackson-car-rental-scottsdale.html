What you need to know

Can I rent an exotic for Barrett-Jackson week?

Yes — we deliver supercars and luxury cars across Scottsdale during auction week. It's our busiest window, so reserve as early as you can.

How early should I book for Barrett-Jackson?

Weeks ahead. Auction-week inventory is limited and books out; early reservations lock in the car and rate.

Do you deliver to the auction or my hotel?

We deliver to your Scottsdale hotel or rental and can coordinate around your schedule for the week.

What does it cost during auction week?

Daily rates start at $250 for the BMW M4 and scale up by vehicle. Popular cars go first, so booking early also protects availability.
