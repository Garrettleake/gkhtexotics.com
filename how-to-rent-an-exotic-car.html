Guide

How to Rent an Exotic Car

GKHT Exotics · Phoenix, Scottsdale & Los Angeles

Renting an exotic car for the first time is simpler than most people expect. There is no membership and no showroom visit — you pick a car online, we confirm it, and we bring it to you. Here is the whole process, start to finish.

1. Pick your car

Browse the full fleet or narrow by city and type — Los Angeles, Scottsdale and Phoenix, or by model like Lamborghini in LA. Each vehicle page shows the daily rate, security deposit, minimum age and what makes that car worth driving.

2. Check the requirements

  • A valid driver’s license in your name.
  • Your own full comprehensive and collision insurance that covers a non-owned vehicle.
  • A refundable security deposit, listed on the vehicle page.
  • You meet the car’s minimum age — many of ours rent at 18+.

3. Send a booking request

Use the booking form or text us with your dates and the car you want. We reply within 24 hours to confirm availability, insurance and deposit. Nothing is locked in until we confirm, so it costs nothing to ask.

4. Delivery and pickup day

We deliver to your hotel, home or venue across the metro area. At handoff we verify your insurance and the deposit, walk the car with you, note any existing marks, and show you how everything works. Then it is yours for the rental period.

Plan a few extra minutes at handoff for the insurance check and walk-around. It is quick, and it protects you as much as us.

5. Returning the car

Bring it back on time, with the same fuel level, inside the mileage allowance and clean. Once it checks out, your deposit is released. That is the whole thing.

First-timer tips

  • Confirm your insurance covers a non-owned exotic before the day — it is the most common delay.
  • If budget matters, start with our affordable rentals.
  • Book high-demand weeks early; the best cars go first.

Ready? Send your first request and we’ll take it from there.

Common questions

How do I rent an exotic car for the first time?

Pick a car on the GKHT site, send a booking request with your dates, and we confirm availability, insurance and deposit within 24 hours. We then deliver the car to you and verify everything at handoff.

Do I have to visit a location?

No. GKHT delivers to your hotel, home or venue across Phoenix, Scottsdale and Los Angeles. The whole booking happens online or by phone.

How far in advance should I book?

As early as you can for popular cars and busy weeks like Barrett-Jackson. For most rentals a few days' notice is enough to verify insurance and schedule delivery.

What happens at delivery?

We verify your insurance and deposit, walk around the car noting any existing marks, show you the controls, and hand over the keys for your rental period.
