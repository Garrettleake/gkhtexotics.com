What you need to know

What's the best car for a bachelor party in Scottsdale?

The Lamborghini Hurácan and Urus, the G63 and the Ferrari 488 are the most-requested. Loud, photogenic and impossible to miss in Old Town.

Can we split one rental across the group?

Yes — many groups book a car for the weekend and share the cost. Any driver on the rental must meet the age and insurance requirements and be listed.

Do you deliver to our Airbnb or hotel?

Yes. We bring the car to your Scottsdale rental, hotel or Old Town venue and pick it up after — no counter, no airport line.

Should we book early for a bachelor weekend?

Definitely. Weekends and event dates go first in Scottsdale. Reserve as early as you can to lock in the cars you want.
