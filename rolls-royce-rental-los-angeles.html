What you need to know

How much does it cost to rent a Rolls-Royce in LA?

Our Los Angeles Rolls-Royces start at $1,500/day for the Ghost. The Cullinan and Spectre are $2,000, and the Cullinan Black Badge is $2,300. Exact rates are on each vehicle page.

Can I rent a Rolls-Royce for a wedding?

Yes — the Ghost and Cullinan are popular wedding cars. We deliver to your venue and can coordinate timing. Ask us when you book.

Do you deliver the Rolls-Royce?

Yes, across Los Angeles, Beverly Hills, West Hollywood, Santa Monica, Malibu and Downtown LA.

What's the difference between the Cullinan and Spectre?

The Cullinan is the SUV with room for the family; the Spectre is a two-door electric grand tourer. Both carry the Starlight headliner.
