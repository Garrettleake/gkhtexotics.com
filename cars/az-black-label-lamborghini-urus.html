About this rental

Rent a Lamborghini Urus in Scottsdale

The Urus is Lamborghini's SUV: five seats, real luggage space, and a twin-turbo V8 that makes it quicker than most sports cars. This one is finished in deep blue, which reads more understated than the usual bright colors and photographs beautifully against the desert. It is the Lamborghini you can take to dinner with four people and still use for the airport run.

Who it’s for

Groups who want the badge without leaving anyone behind, golf trips, resort stays in Paradise Valley, and anyone who needs a supercar that also does errands. The most practical exotic in our Phoenix fleet. See every vehicle we rent to drivers under 25.

Delivery in Phoenix & Scottsdale

This vehicle is based in Scottsdale and delivered to your hotel, home or venue across Phoenix, Scottsdale, Paradise Valley, Tempe, Mesa, Chandler and Old Town, with airport handoffs at Sky Harbor (PHX) and Scottsdale Airport (SDL). Delivery outside the metro area is available — fees may apply, so ask when you book. Browse every SUV rental in Phoenix & Scottsdale, or see the full Phoenix & Scottsdale fleet.

Deposit, age and insurance

You’ll need a valid driver’s license, full comprehensive and collision insurance in your name, and a refundable security deposit, confirmed when you book. Minimum age for this vehicle is 21+.
