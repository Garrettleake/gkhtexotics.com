Before You Book

Your own insurance is the only coverage

We do not provide insurance to renters. You must carry, and prove before pickup, liability of at least 100/300/100 (or $300,000 combined single limit) and physical damage coverage that applies to a non-owned vehicle and pays at least $50,000.

We verify this by calling your insurer with you on the line before releasing the car, so please allow 15 minutes at pickup. Many personal policies cap non-owned physical damage well below their stated collision limit — ask your insurer specifically what they would pay on a $50,000 non-owned vehicle before you book.

Security deposit

$800 on a credit card, $1,600 on a debit card. Debit deposits carry more risk and take longer to recover, which is why paying by credit card costs you less.

The deposit is charged, not held, and refunded after the return inspection. Expect it back within roughly a week rather than the same day. If anything is deducted we send an itemised statement with photos and receipts.

Who can rent

Minimum age 18, with a valid unexpired licence held for at least one full year. No permits or restricted licences. The renter must be the driver, and any additional drivers have to be listed on the agreement. The card must be in the renter's own name.

Where you can drive

Arizona only — the vehicle may not leave the state. It is GPS tracked, and speed and acceleration are recorded continuously.

Delivery & taxes

Pickup and drop-off at our location is free. Delivery to you is $50 and collection is $50. Arizona Transaction Privilege Tax (11.100%) and the Maricopa County rental car surcharge (3.25%) are added on top of the daily rate.

Caring for this car

The finish is BMW Individual Frozen Red Metallic II. It cannot be polished or waxed and must never go through an automatic car wash — hand wash only, with matte-safe products.

Ground clearance is low, so take driveways, ramps and speed bumps slowly and at an angle. The semi-slick tyres have very limited wet grip. The front-mounted intakes mean you must never drive through standing water. There is only one key fob and no spare.
