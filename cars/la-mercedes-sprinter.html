About this rental

Chauffeured Mercedes Sprinter Van in Los Angeles

A Mercedes Sprinter with a professional driver included, booked by the hour with a four-hour minimum. It is the answer for moving a group around Los Angeles without splitting into three cars — the driver handles the freeways and the parking while everyone else sits together in the back. Ideal for nights out, event days, and airport runs for a whole party.

Who it’s for

Bachelor and bachelorette parties, wedding logistics, corporate groups, concert and game-day transport, and airport pickups for large groups. Bookable at 18+ since you are not the one driving.

Service area

Delivered to your hotel, residence, set or venue across Los Angeles, Beverly Hills, West Hollywood, Santa Monica, Malibu and Downtown LA, with airport handoffs at LAX, Van Nuys (VNY) and Hollywood Burbank (BUR). Delivery outside the metro area is available — fees may apply, so ask when you book. See the full Los Angeles fleet.

Driver, deposit and age

A professional driver is included, so no driver’s license or insurance is required from you and there is no security deposit. Bookings are open to guests 18 and over. Billed by the hour with a four-hour minimum.

How to book

Call or text (213) 845-5211, or send a request through our booking page. We confirm availability, insurance and deposit within 24 hours, seven days a week.
