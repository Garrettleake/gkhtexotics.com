About this rental

Rent a Rolls-Royce Ghost in Los Angeles

The Ghost is the Rolls-Royce sedan — smaller than a Phantom, larger than anything else, and finished here with Black Badge trim for a darker, more contemporary look. The ride is the point: the car simply disconnects you from the road surface. For a Los Angeles evening that needs a Rolls-Royce but not an SUV, the Ghost is the answer.

Who it’s for

Weddings, executive dinners, anniversary evenings, and anyone who wants the classic Rolls-Royce sedan experience with a modern edge. Available at 18+ with the right insurance. See every vehicle we rent to drivers under 25.

Delivery in Los Angeles

Delivered to your hotel, residence, set or venue across Los Angeles, Beverly Hills, West Hollywood, Santa Monica, Malibu and Downtown LA, with airport handoffs at LAX, Van Nuys (VNY) and Hollywood Burbank (BUR). Delivery outside the metro area is available — fees may apply, so ask when you book. Browse every luxury car rental in Los Angeles, or see the full Los Angeles fleet.
