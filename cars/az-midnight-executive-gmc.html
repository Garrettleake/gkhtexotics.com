About this rental

Chauffeured GMC Black Car Service in Phoenix & Scottsdale

A driven black-car service in a full-size GMC SUV, billed by the hour. You do not drive; a professional driver picks you up, takes you where you are going, and waits. It is the right answer for airport runs, evenings where nobody wants to be the designated driver, and moving a group between venues without organizing three rideshares.

Who it’s for

Airport transfers to and from Sky Harbor and Scottsdale Airport, dinners and nights out, corporate transport, golf-day logistics, and wedding parties. Bookable at 18+ since you are not the one driving.

Service area

This vehicle is based in Scottsdale and delivered to your hotel, home or venue across Phoenix, Scottsdale, Paradise Valley, Tempe, Mesa, Chandler and Old Town, with airport handoffs at Sky Harbor (PHX) and Scottsdale Airport (SDL). Delivery outside the metro area is available — fees may apply, so ask when you book. See the full Phoenix & Scottsdale fleet.

Driver, deposit and age

A professional driver is included, so no driver’s license or insurance is required from you and there is no security deposit. Bookings are open to guests 18 and over. Billed by the hour at the rate shown above, plus the pickup fee and per-mile charge listed in the specs.

How to book

Send your dates through the booking form or text (213) 845-5211. Availability is confirmed within 24 hours, and the vehicle is released once insurance and deposit are verified.
