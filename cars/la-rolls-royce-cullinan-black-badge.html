About this rental

Rent a 2026 Rolls-Royce Cullinan Black Badge in Los Angeles

Black Badge is Rolls-Royce's darker, sharper edition of the Cullinan: black chrome, dark wheels, a more assertive chassis tune, and a cabin with a technical rather than traditional finish. It is the Cullinan for people who find the standard car a touch too formal. In Los Angeles, it is the version that gets requested most.

Music-industry and entertainment clients, nights out, content shoots, and anyone who wants the most current Rolls-Royce look available. Available at 18+ with the right insurance. See every vehicle we rent to drivers under 25.
