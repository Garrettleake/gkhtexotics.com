About this rental

Rent a Cadillac Escalade in Scottsdale

The current Escalade is the best version of the American luxury SUV: a huge curved display across the dash, a cabin that seats seven in real comfort, and Super Cruise hands-free driving on compatible highways. It is the vehicle that hotel valets and event venues recognize on sight. For moving a group around the Valley in comfort, it is hard to beat.

Who it’s for

Family trips, corporate groups, airport pickups for a party of six, and anyone staying at a resort who wants a vehicle that matches the setting. Flexible on age for qualified drivers — ask us.

Delivery in Phoenix & Scottsdale

This vehicle is based in Scottsdale and delivered to your hotel, home or venue across Phoenix, Scottsdale, Paradise Valley, Tempe, Mesa, Chandler and Old Town, with airport handoffs at Sky Harbor (PHX) and Scottsdale Airport (SDL). Delivery outside the metro area is available — fees may apply, so ask when you book. Browse every luxury car rental in Phoenix & Scottsdale, or see the full Phoenix & Scottsdale fleet.

Deposit, age and insurance

You’ll need a valid driver’s license, full comprehensive and collision insurance in your name, and a refundable security deposit of $1,000. Minimum age is 25, with flexibility for qualified younger drivers — ask us when you book. We verify insurance and the deposit before the car is released.
